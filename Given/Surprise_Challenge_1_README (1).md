# Surprise Challenge - 1

## AIML

### PS01 – Surprise Challenge 1: Spot the Difference

You'll be given two models that look very similar — they agree on most inputs you try.  
But they're not identical: on some specific kind of input, they give different answers. Just checking a few random inputs and seeing them agree isn't enough — your system needs to actively dig deeper and find where they actually disagree, if anywhere.

## Requirements:

- Say clearly whether the two models are the same, different, or "look similar but I'm not fully sure."
- Show the specific inputs (or type of input) where the two models gave different answers — not just an overall "they agree X% of the time" number.
- Explain how you searched for the difference — did you just try random inputs, or did you specifically look for tricky cases?
- **What we're checking:** does your system correctly find the real difference between the two models, and can it show us exactly how it found it (which inputs, what pattern) — not just a confident-sounding guess with no evidence behind it.
